# Resume Delta — Current vs. Concentra-Targeted
## What Changed and Why

This document tracks every meaningful change between Bryan's current working resume and the Concentra VP AI tailored version (RESUME_CONCENTRA.md), with the rationale for each change.

---

## Strategic Changes

### 1. Title / Headline
- **Current:** `Head of AI | AI Platform & Agentic Engineering Leader | Former CTO`
- **Concentra version:** `VP, Artificial Intelligence | Healthcare AI Governance & Enterprise Strategy | Former CTO`
- **Why:** Concentra is hiring for a VP role. The title should mirror the role level. "Healthcare AI Governance" signals alignment with their primary concern. "Agentic Engineering Leader" is accurate but sounds more technical than executive for this audience.

### 2. Healthcare Added to Executive Impact
- **Current:** "federal, cybersecurity, education, retail, and industrial sectors"
- **Concentra version:** "healthcare, federal, cybersecurity, education, retail, and industrial sectors"
- **Why:** Healthcare is the first thing a Concentra recruiter will scan for. Omitting it from the top section when Bryan has deployed healthcare AI is a missed opportunity. Healthcare belongs at the front of that list.

### 3. HIPAA Published Article Added to Executive Impact
- **Current:** Not mentioned anywhere in the resume
- **Concentra version:** Added to Executive Impact bullets: "Published practitioner-level guidance on HIPAA AI data governance"
- **Why:** A published author on HIPAA AI governance is a significant differentiator for a healthcare organization. This should be visible at the top, not buried or absent.

### 4. Summary Reoriented to Healthcare First
- **Current summary:** Leads with "production-grade platforms across infrastructure, cloud, and advanced AI systems" and "GenAI, agentic architectures, AI governance"
- **Concentra version:** Leads with "healthcare, federal, cybersecurity..." and "HIPAA, SOX, GDPR, CMMC, FERPA"
- **Why:** The first two sentences of a VP-level resume determine whether the reader continues. For Concentra, healthcare regulatory language must come first.

### 5. Healthcare AI Representative Engagement Added
- **Current:** Cybersecurity, Government, Industrial Safety, Retail — no healthcare
- **Concentra version:** Added first: "Healthcare AI Platform: HIPAA-governed AI with PHI classification, RAG-powered knowledge retrieval, and audit trail — mental health/therapeutic support application"
- **Why:** The Therapeutic AI Companion is a real healthcare deployment. It needs to be on the resume. Concentra will look for healthcare work — if it’s not visible, they’ll assume it doesn’t exist.

### 6. Healthcare AI & HIPAA Section Added to Inflexis Experience
- **Current:** Governance, Risk & Compliance section mentions HIPAA but doesn’t lead with healthcare
- **Concentra version:** New dedicated subsection: "Healthcare AI & HIPAA Governance" appears first in the Inflexis experience block
- **Why:** Subsection ordering signals priority. For Concentra, HIPAA and healthcare governance should be the first thing they read about your Inflexis work.

### 7. HIPAA Published Article Added as Standalone Section
- **Current:** Not on resume
- **Concentra version:** Published Thought Leadership table included, with HIPAA article listed first
- **Why:** Thought leadership is a VP-level differentiator. A published HIPAA AI article is a rare credential for a VP AI candidate — it signals regulatory literacy that most candidates can only claim through coursework or vendor certifications.

### 8. Core Capabilities Reordered and Reframed
- **Current:** Enterprise AI Strategy, Agentic AI, GenAI/RAG, AI Governance, Platform Engineering, IaC/DevSecOps...
- **Concentra version:** Leads with Healthcare AI Governance, Responsible AI/Ethics, AI Risk Management, HIPAA/FDA Regulatory...
- **Why:** Capability sections are scanned, not read. The first 4 items are what a skimming recruiter sees. For Concentra, those need to be healthcare-first.

### 9. AI Portfolio Management Added
- **Current:** Not explicit in capabilities or experience
- **Concentra version:** Added to Core Capabilities: "AI Portfolio Management & Prioritization"
- **Why:** The Concentra job description specifically calls out "maintain a prioritized AI portfolio." This language should appear on the resume.

### 10. FDA AI/ML Guidance — Made Explicit
- **Current:** Not mentioned
- **Concentra version:** Added to HIPAA section in Inflexis experience; added to Core Capabilities; added to Technical Expertise section
- **Why:** The job description specifically mentions "FDA guidance on AI/ML-based Software." Demonstrating awareness of this is table stakes for the role.

### 11. Vendor Management Made Explicit
- **Current:** Implied through multi-LLM platform build
- **Concentra version:** Explicitly stated: "Managed AI vendor relationships across 6 LLM providers"
- **Why:** The job description calls out "Evaluate and manage relationships with AI technology vendors." This needs to be visible language on the resume.
