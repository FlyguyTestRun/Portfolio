# K-12 AI Governance Program

## Context

A multi-district education initiative needed an AI adoption roadmap that balanced instructional value, administrative efficiency, and student data protection.

## Problem

- Fragmented systems and inconsistent data handling across districts.
- High sensitivity around FERPA/CIPA controls and audit expectations.
- Limited technical bandwidth for district-wide AI architecture design.

## Architecture Approach

- Designed a phased adoption model with governance controls at each phase.
- Implemented role-scoped access patterns and policy-aware knowledge workflows.
- Structured retrieval and response patterns for school operations and administrative use cases.

## Outcomes

- Delivered a repeatable architecture framework usable across district variants.
- Improved stakeholder alignment between leadership, IT, and program owners.
- Reduced risk by embedding governance before broad AI feature rollout.

## Stack And Governance

- Azure-aligned identity and platform controls
- RAG and retrieval governance patterns
- Deterministic policy checks and human-in-the-loop approvals
