# Critical Infrastructure OT Security Intelligence Platform

## Context

A safety-critical operations environment required AI-assisted monitoring and decision support while maintaining strict control over risk and deployment boundaries.

## Problem

- OT telemetry and threat signals were difficult to operationalize quickly.
- Security and compliance requirements limited tolerance for opaque AI behavior.
- Deployment constraints required patterns suitable for restricted environments.

## Architecture Approach

- Built a governed multi-agent flow for telemetry interpretation and incident narration.
- Added deterministic compliance controls before model-assisted reasoning steps.
- Designed architecture patterns that support offline and air-gapped operation modes.

## Outcomes

- Improved speed and consistency of operational threat interpretation.
- Increased trust with explicit controls, traceability, and approval boundaries.
- Enabled deployment options aligned with high-assurance environment constraints.

## Stack And Governance

- Multi-agent orchestration with stage-level controls
- Deterministic policy enforcement and audit trail support
- Containerized interfaces and API-based integration patterns
