# Government Contractor Workflow Intelligence

## Context

A government contracting workflow required better contract intelligence, communications support, and compliance-conscious process automation.

## Problem

- Contract and procurement workflows were document-heavy and slow to analyze.
- Regulatory expectations required strong auditability and controlled approvals.
- Teams needed reliable AI support integrated with existing execution flows.

## Architecture Approach

- Designed RAG-assisted contract and procurement retrieval workflows.
- Added durable, step-based execution with review gates for regulated actions.
- Built traceable workflow outputs with role-scoped access and audit logging.

## Outcomes

- Improved turnaround time for contract intelligence and communication summaries.
- Strengthened governance posture through explicit controls and review points.
- Created a reusable architecture pattern for regulated workflow environments.

## Stack And Governance

- API-first workflow integration
- Role-scoped access controls and policy checks
- Citation-grounded retrieval and auditable action records
