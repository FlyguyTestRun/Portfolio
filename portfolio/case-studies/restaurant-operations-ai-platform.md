# Restaurant Operations AI Platform

## Context

A multi-location food service operation needed better inventory, pricing, and operational forecasting using existing POS and business systems.

## Problem

- Decisions relied on manual reporting cycles with delayed insights.
- Pricing and inventory changes lacked a consistent intelligence layer.
- Teams needed actionable outputs without replacing core operational systems.

## Architecture Approach

- Integrated transaction and operational data into a governed intelligence workflow.
- Built modular agents for pricing analysis, demand signals, and operational summaries.
- Implemented retrieval-backed response patterns with business-context grounding.

## Outcomes

- Faster operational insight cycles for store-level and leadership decisions.
- Better margin visibility through structured pricing intelligence patterns.
- Reusable architecture blueprint for additional franchise and food-service programs.

## Stack And Governance

- API-driven ingestion from operational and POS-aligned systems
- Hybrid retrieval with business-context grounding
- Governance controls for repeatable, auditable outputs
